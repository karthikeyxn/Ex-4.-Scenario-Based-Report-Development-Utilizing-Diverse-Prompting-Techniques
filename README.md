## Objective:

The goal of this experiment is to design and develop an AI-powered chatbot that can handle customer inquiries, provide product support, and enhance customer experience in a retail environment.
Different prompt engineering techniques will be used to improve chatbot performance, clarity, and response quality.

## Aim:

To explore and apply various AI prompting techniques—including Straightforward Prompts, Tabular Format Prompts, Missing Word Prompts, and Preceding Question Prompts—to guide the development, testing, and optimization of an AI chatbot for customer support in a retail scenario.

1. Straightforward Prompt Technique
Algorithm:

Identify a direct customer question.

Frame a clear, unambiguous instruction for the chatbot.

Collect and analyze the response for accuracy and relevance.

Prompt:

“Provide details about the return policy for damaged products.”

## Output:

“Our return policy allows customers to return damaged products within 7 days of delivery. Please keep the invoice and product packaging for verification.”

## Result:

Straightforward prompting produced quick and precise customer support responses suitable for standard FAQs.

2. Tabular Format Prompting Technique
## Algorithm:

Determine information that benefits from structured comparison.

Instruct the chatbot to organize responses in a tabular layout.

Evaluate response readability and completeness.

Prompt:

“Create a comparison table of our delivery options including mode, delivery time, and shipping cost.”

## Output:
| Delivery Mode     | Estimated Time                  | Shipping Cost |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Standard Delivery | 4–6 business days               | Free          |
| Express Delivery  | 1–2 business days               | ₹150          |
| Same-Day Delivery | Within 24 hours (select cities) | ₹250          |

## Result:

Tabular prompting improved clarity and organization, making it easier for customers to compare options.

3. Missing Word Prompting Technique
## Algorithm:

Frame an incomplete sentence to test chatbot comprehension.

Leave a key word blank intentionally.

Observe if the chatbot infers the correct context.

Prompt:

“Our loyalty program rewards customers with points for every purchase, which can be redeemed for ______.”

## Output:

“…which can be redeemed for discounts and exclusive offers.”

## Result:

Missing Word prompting validated the chatbot’s ability to understand context and infer meaningful completions, improving natural interaction quality.

4. Preceding Question Prompting Technique
## Algorithm:

Ask a contextual setup question followed by a dependent instruction.

Ensure the chatbot connects both prompts logically.

Evaluate reasoning and flow of information.

Prompt:

Q1: “What types of payment options are available?”
Q2: “Based on that, which method would you recommend for fast checkout?”

## Output:

Q1 Answer: “We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, wallets, and cash on delivery.”

Q2 Answer: “For fast checkout, UPI and digital wallet payments are recommended as they process instantly.”

## Result:

Preceding question prompting enabled the chatbot to deliver context-aware, sequential responses, improving dialogue continuity.

## Overall Result Summary
| Prompt Type               | Purpose               | Chatbot Response Quality | Suitable Use Case     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Straightforward Prompt    | Direct answers        | High accuracy            | FAQs and policies     |
| Tabular Format Prompt     | Structured comparison | Very high clarity        | Product/delivery info |
| Missing Word Prompt       | Context understanding | High relevance           | Natural conversation  |
| Preceding Question Prompt | Context continuity    | Excellent logical flow   | Multi-turn dialogue   |

## Final Result:

By applying diverse prompting techniques, the chatbot was able to:

Respond accurately to user queries.

Structure information effectively.

Exhibit contextual understanding.

Maintain logical flow across multiple turns.

Thus, prompt engineering significantly enhanced the chatbot’s ability to provide efficient and user-friendly retail customer support.
